# Data Sources & Legal

ChartHorizon is a **local-first analysis tool**. It ships with **no market data**.
When you run it, it fetches data **to your own machine** for your **personal use**;
nothing is redistributed by this project.

## Sources

- **CFTC** (Commitments of Traders, Open Interest) — U.S. government public domain.
- **Yahoo Finance via the `yfinance` library** (prices, contracts, volume, seasonal
  history) — fetched locally for personal use. ChartHorizon does **not** redistribute
  this data. You are responsible for complying with the data providers' terms of use.

ChartHorizon is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** Yahoo, CME, ICE,
or any exchange or data provider.
